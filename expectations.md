## Participation

Students should participate actively in lectures, labs, discussions, and applied practice.

Come prepared to ask questions, complete in-class work, and engage with the material.

## Professionalism

Treat classmates, staff, and guest speakers with respect.

Communicate clearly, follow classroom norms, and represent yourself in a way that reflects workplace readiness.

## Assignments

Complete required assignments by the stated due dates.

Track weekly work in the [Assignments](assignments/README.md) section and each week's schedule page.

## Certification Work

Certification preparation is a core part of this program.

Students are expected to complete assigned CertMaster work and related study activities on schedule.

See [Certification Information](certification/README.md) for details.

## Asking for Help

Ask for help early when you are stuck.

Use class time, course resources, and [one-on-one meetings](one-on-one-links.md) to get support.
